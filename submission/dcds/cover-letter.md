Dear Editors of Discrete and Continuous Dynamical Systems,

Please consider the manuscript

“Fredholm sensitivity to constrained delayed coupling in networks of RFDEs”

for publication as a research article in Discrete and Continuous Dynamical
Systems.

The paper identifies a Fredholm sensitivity mechanism that remains after a
delayed-coupling perturbation is annihilated by the stationary projection at
every history.  We prove a norm-optimal two-delay right inverse, an exact
range theorem under prescribed matrix patterns, and bounds uniform in the
network size.  We then realize the resulting functional in a fast--slow RFDE
fold problem.  The paper distinguishes the reusable operator mechanism from
the model-specific fold calculation and includes explicit growing network
families showing both persistence and loss of uniformity.

The transfer of this coefficient to a heteroclinic parameter is presented
separately as a conditional application.  It is not part of the paper's
unconditional existence claims.

The manuscript fits DCDS through its combination of retarded functional
differential equations, fast--slow dynamics, Fredholm solvability, invariant
manifolds, and network-size-uniform estimates.  The analytic results do not
depend on numerical data.  The illustrative computation, source files, and
reproduction instructions are publicly available in the versioned release
linked in the manuscript.

OpenAI Codex use is disclosed in the manuscript.  The author remains
responsible for every equation, proof, citation, computation, and statement.
