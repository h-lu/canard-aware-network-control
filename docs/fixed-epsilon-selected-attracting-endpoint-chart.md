# Fixed-\(\varepsilon\) attracting-endpoint geometry: a rank-two compatibility audit

Status: **the solution-manifold geometry and the finite dimension obstruction
are proved exactly; a selected attracting endpoint chart is not
constructed.**  In the proposed \(p=6\), 16-history-cell discretization,

\[
 m_h=2(16\cdot6+1)=194.
\]

The two RFDE endpoint-compatibility equations have exact rank two on this
raw \(C^0\) coefficient space.  Consequently its discrete
endpoint-compatible level has dimension 192.  This 194-dimensional space is
not a global-\(C^1\) discretization of the classical solution manifold: the
30 internal derivative-continuity equations would lower the dimensions to
164 and 162, respectively.  A 193-dimensional fixed-parameter immersion
**inside** either compatible level is impossible.

This does not forbid an ambient 193-coordinate endpoint parameterization.
It can be used only if the two compatibility equations remain explicit BVP
rows.  A projected-history repair then preserves the advertised
\(775\)-by-\(774\) arithmetic count, but the existing candidate has not
constructed the required selected chart, projections, invariant foliation,
or Fredholm operator.  The exact audit is implemented in
[fixed_epsilon_selected_attracting_endpoint_chart.py](../src/canard_control/fixed_epsilon_selected_attracting_endpoint_chart.py),
generated by
[fixed_epsilon_selected_attracting_endpoint_chart.py](../experiments/fixed_epsilon_selected_attracting_endpoint_chart.py),
and tested in
[test_fixed_epsilon_selected_attracting_endpoint_chart.py](../tests/test_fixed_epsilon_selected_attracting_endpoint_chart.py).

**Continuous-scale correction.**  The rank-two \(194\to192\) result is an
exact fixed-mesh algebraic theorem, not the dimension of a closed
codimension-two subset of \(W^{1,p}\).  The later
[sliding-window/\(W^{1,p}\) bridge](fixed-epsilon-sliding-window-w1p-bridge.md)

conforming there without derivative seams.  On the preferred
fixed-parameter scale, a selected attracting trace should be defined

## 1. Exact continuous compatibility map

Use the fold-time history
\(\phi=(x,y)\in C^1([-H,0],\mathbb R^2)\), where the enlarged horizon is
\(H=\Theta_*>5\).  At the candidate midpoint,

\[
 H_{\rm diag}=7.397086298188131.
\]

For fixed \((\nu,\eta)\), the quadratic RFDE vector field at the current
endpoint is

\[
\begin{aligned}
 F_1(\phi;\nu,\eta)={}&y_0-x_0^2
 +\delta\left[-\frac{x_0^3}{3}
 +\frac15\left(\frac{x_4+x_5}{2}-x_0\right)\right]\\
 &+\delta^2\eta(x_0^2-x_H^2)
 +\frac{\delta^3}{4}
 \left(\frac{x_4^3+x_5^3}{2}-x_0^3\right),\\
 F_2(\phi;\nu,\eta)={}&-x_0+\delta\nu,
 \qquad \delta=\frac1{\sqrt5},
\end{aligned}
\tag{1.1}
\]

where \(x_j=x(-j)\) and \(x_H=x(-H)\).  The classical compatibility map is

\[
 C(\phi;\nu,\eta)=
 \begin{pmatrix}x'(0)-F_1(\phi;\nu,\eta)\\
 y'(0)-F_2(\phi;\nu,\eta)
 \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{1.2}
\]

Thus the strong solution manifold is

\[
 \mathcal M_{\nu,\eta}=C(\,\cdot\,;\nu,\eta)^{-1}(0).
\tag{1.3}
\]

For this fixed-delay polynomial RFDE, \(C\) is smooth on \(C^1\).  The
rank argument below is self-contained; the general codimension-\(n\)
solution-manifold framework is consistent with
[Walther (2003)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jde.2003.07.001).
For the continuous statement one may choose any terminal bump width
\(0<\ell<4\), so no decimal approximation of \(H\) is needed.  The
specific choice \(\ell=H_{\rm diag}/16\) below belongs to the pinned finite
coefficient audit.

## 2. An exact discrete rank-two witness

Let \(E_N\) denote the two-component continuous piecewise-polynomial space
of degree at most six on 16 cells, with function values but not derivatives
identified at the 15 internal joins.  Thus \(E_N\) is globally \(C^0\),
piecewise smooth, and has a one-sided polynomial derivative at the current
endpoint.  Its dimension is

\[
 \dim E_N=2\{16(6)+1\}=194.
\tag{2.0}
\]

The map \(C_N\) is the discrete endpoint evaluation of (1.2).  Its zero set
is called the **discrete endpoint-compatible level** below.  It is not, by
that name alone, a discretization inside the full classical \(C^1\)
solution manifold.

Let \(\ell=H_{\rm diag}/16\) be the last-cell width and define the continuous
piecewise cubic

\[
 b(\theta)=
 \begin{cases}
 0,&-H_{\rm diag}\leq\theta\leq-\ell,\\[2mm]
 \displaystyle\frac{\theta(\theta+\ell)^2}{\ell^2},
     &-\ell\leq\theta\leq0.
 \end{cases}
\tag{2.1}
\]

It belongs to the degree-six piecewise history space and satisfies

\[
 b(-\ell)=b'(-\ell)=b(0)=0,
 \qquad b'(0)=1.
\tag{2.2}
\]

Since \(\ell<4\), the support of \(b\) misses all three delayed evaluation
points \(-4,-5,-H_{\rm diag}\).  Put

\[
 e_x=(b,0),\qquad e_y=(0,b).
\tag{2.3}
\]

These directions change neither the current values nor any delayed value;
they change only the two endpoint derivatives.  Hence, for every history and
every parameter pair,

\[
 C(\phi+\alpha e_x+\beta e_y;\nu,\eta)
 =C(\phi;\nu,\eta)+\binom\alpha\beta.
\tag{2.4}
\]

In particular,

\[
 D_\phi C(\phi;\nu,\eta)[e_x\ e_y]=I_2.
\tag{2.5}
\]

This proves surjectivity without a numerical rank computation and without
using a particular candidate orbit.

> **Theorem 2.1 (discrete endpoint-compatible dimension and the narrow
> no-go).**  Let \(E_N\) be the raw \(C^0\) piecewise-polynomial history
> space above.  Then
> \(\dim E_N=194\), the restriction \(C_N:E_N\to\mathbb R^2\) is a
> submersion, and its discrete endpoint-compatible level
> \(C_N^{-1}(0)\) is locally 192-dimensional.  There is no
> \(C^1\) map
> \(\Gamma:U\subset\mathbb R^{193}\to E_N\) such that
> \(C_N(\Gamma(\xi);\nu,\eta)=0\) for fixed parameters and
> \(\operatorname{rank}D_\xi\Gamma=193\).  If global \(C^1\) continuity is
> imposed at the 15 internal joins, the history subspace has dimension 164,
> its endpoint-compatible level has dimension 162, and the same
> nonexistence conclusion holds.

**Proof.**  The scalar coefficient count is \(16\cdot6+1=97\), so the
two-component count is 194.  Equation (2.5) gives
\(\operatorname{rank}D C_N=2\); the regular-level theorem gives dimension
\(194-2=192\).  If the stated immersion existed, differentiating
\(C_N\circ\Gamma=0\) would put a 193-dimensional image of
\(D\Gamma\) inside the 192-dimensional kernel of \(D C_N\), a
contradiction. \(\square\)

For the last statement, derivative continuity adds one independent equation
at each of 15 joins for each of two state components.  Hence the dimensions
are \(194-30=164\) and \(164-2=162\).  The witness (2.1) satisfies both
value and derivative matching at its only nonzero join, so (2.5) remains a
rank-two right inverse on that global-\(C^1\) subspace.

The same bump directions also give the exact smooth retraction

\[
 \mathcal R_{\nu,\eta}(\phi)
 =\phi-C_1(\phi;\nu,\eta)e_x
       -C_2(\phi;\nu,\eta)e_y,
\tag{2.6}
\]

because \(C(\mathcal R_{\nu,\eta}\phi;\nu,\eta)=0\) identically.  Thus
compatibility itself is constructible, smoothly in \((\nu,\eta)\).  What
(2.6) does **not** supply is attraction, invariance, an outer selection, a
stable foliation, or a canonical old-history extension.

## 3. How an ambient 193-coordinate chart can be counted correctly

Theorem 2.1 rules out only the interpretation

\[
 \Gamma_-:\mathbb R^{193}\longrightarrow\mathcal M_{\nu,\eta}
 \quad\text{with rank }193.
\tag{3.1}
\]

It does not rule out an ambient map

\[
 \Gamma_-^{\rm amb}:\mathbb R^{193}\longrightarrow E_N
\tag{3.2}
\]

followed by the two residual equations

\[
 C_N(\Gamma_-^{\rm amb}(\xi_-);\nu,\eta)=0.
\tag{3.3}
\]

If (3.3) is transverse, its admissible coordinate set has dimension
\(193-2=191\).  This is a codimension-one trace inside the
192-dimensional discrete endpoint-compatible level, exactly the geometry
that the old shorthand was trying to express.  Calling (3.2) itself a
“193-dimensional compatible chart” is nevertheless false.

There is a count-consistent finite BVP design.  Replace each raw 194-row
entry, exit, and seam history equality by 192 projected history rows and add
six explicit compatibility rows.  The exact ledger is

| ambient attracting coordinates \(\xi_-\) | \(193\) |
| compatible repelling coordinate \(\xi_+\) | \(1\) |

In this design, compatibility of \(\Gamma_+\) is a construction hypothesis;

This table is an exact arithmetic repair, not a constructed endpoint

## 4. Phase and time tangent

If \(x_t\) is a \(C^2\) classical solution, then

\[
 C(x_t;\nu,\eta)=0.
\]

Differentiation in time gives

\[
 D_\phi C(x_t;\nu,\eta)\,\dot x_t=0.
\tag{4.1}
\]

The translation tangent is therefore already a direction in the continuous
compatible tangent space.  It does not add an external compatibility
direction.  The later condition \(V^-(0)=1\) removes the global translation
freedom of the two-branch BVP; it cannot repair a missing endpoint
compatibility dimension.

For the repaired ambient-chart reading, the transverse solution of (3.3)
has dimension 191.  If that trace is invariant and contains one independent
time tangent, at most 190 additional fibre directions remain in that trace.
This is only a dimension statement: existence and attraction of those
fibres are still open.

## 5. Parameter derivatives and the old-history obstruction

For a parameter-coherent compatible chart \(\Gamma\), differentiating

\[
 C(\Gamma(\xi;\nu,\eta);\nu,\eta)=0
\tag{5.1}
\]

gives the necessary identities

\[
\begin{aligned}
 D_\phi C\,\Gamma_{\xi_j}&=0,\\
 D_\phi C\,\Gamma_\nu&=-C_\nu
   =\binom{0}{\delta},\\
 D_\phi C\,\Gamma_\eta&=-C_\eta
   =\binom{\delta^2(x_0^2-x_H^2)}0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{5.2}
\]

For \(\lambda,\mu\in\{\nu,\eta\}\), the second jet must also satisfy

\[
\begin{aligned}
 D_\phi C\,\Gamma_{\lambda\mu}
 &+D_{\phi\phi}^2C[\Gamma_\lambda,\Gamma_\mu]
 +D_{\phi\lambda}^2C\,\Gamma_\mu
 +D_{\phi\mu}^2C\,\Gamma_\lambda
 +C_{\lambda\mu}=0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{5.3}
\]

These are endpoint identities, not a proof of selectedness.

The enlarged interval \([-H,-5)\) creates a sharper obstruction.  At
\(\eta=0\), the base RFDE uses only delays 4 and 5, so this older interval is
dynamically inert.  Nevertheless \(F_\eta\) reads \(x(-H)\).

This can be seen inside the same finite history space.  The first cell ends
at

\[
 -H_{\rm diag}+\ell=-\frac{15}{16}H_{\rm diag}<-5.
\]

On that cell put

\[
 q(\theta)=
 \left(1-\frac{\theta+H_{\rm diag}}\ell\right)^2,
\tag{5.4}
\]

and extend it by zero afterwards.  Then \(q(-H_{\rm diag})=1\) and
\(q=q'=0\) at the first-cell join.  At \((\nu,\eta)=(0,0)\), both histories

\[
 \phi_0=(0,0),\qquad \phi_1=(q,0)
\tag{5.5}
\]

are compatible and have identical recent histories on \([-5,0]\).  Their
\(\eta=0\) future current-state trajectories and active histories are
identical.  The enlarged history states still carry the shifted inert tail
until it ages out, so this is not equality of those phase-space states.  But

\[
 F_\eta(\phi_0)=\binom00,
 \qquad
 F_\eta(\phi_1)=\binom{-1/5}{0}.
\tag{5.6}
\]

Therefore the \(\eta=0\) semiflow does not select the \(\eta\)-jet of an
enlarged-horizon endpoint chart.  A complete-orbit, flow-hull, or other
declared old-history normalization is necessary; the artificial entry
template is one smooth choice, but no current theorem identifies it as the
selected attracting choice.

## 6. What is theorem, certificate, and open RFDE analysis

The exact theorem layer consists of:

- the raw \(C^0\) count \(194\), rank-two discrete endpoint compatibility,
  and discrete level dimension \(192\);
- the global-\(C^1\) count \(164\) and endpoint-compatible dimension \(162\);
- nonexistence of a rank-193 immersion inside that level;
- the explicit smooth compatibility retraction (2.6);
- the parameter identities (5.2)--(5.3);
- the old-history pair (5.5)--(5.6); and
- the repaired \(775\)-by-\(774\) arithmetic ledger.

The machine-readable certificate is a finite symbolic audit of these
identities at the pinned diagnostic horizon.  It is not interval propagation
of the exact period \(T_*\), a collocation inverse, or a numerical invariant
bundle computation.

The continuous compatibility level in \(C^1\) is a smooth codimension-two
solution manifold by the same split-surjectivity proof.  What remains open
in the continuous RFDE is materially stronger:

1. construct the selected attracting history trace by an invariant
   foliation, Lyapunov--Perron map, or continuation theorem at
   \(\delta=1/\sqrt5\);
2. normalize the full old interval in a way coherent in \((\nu,\eta)\);
3. prove the first and second parameter jets and the required stable
   dichotomy bounds;
4. construct the compatible backward-extendible repelling trace;
5. instantiate the projected entry/exit/seam maps and prove the one-cokernel
   Fredholm property; and
6. only then validate the nonlinear BVP, adjoint, tails, and root response.
