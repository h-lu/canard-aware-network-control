# Stage 5A: correlated center-parameter jet pilot

Status: **source-bound binary64 mesh-refinement diagnostic.  It is not a
directed coefficient enclosure, order-five remainder bound, Route-C event,
complete-history family, stable-sheet intersection, onset, or routing
theorem.**

The wide-family calculation showed that shrinking symmetric state shards
does not cure the old zero-centered first/second variation majorants.  The
present pilot instead integrates the five correlated factorial coefficients
together at

\[
 J_0=2409/8000=0.301125,
 \qquad
 z(t,J_0+\delta)=\sum_{k=0}^{4}a_k(t)\delta^k+R_5,
 \quad a_k=z_k/k!.
\]

The ten-dimensional solver state is ordered as
\((a_{0,v},a_{0,w},\ldots,a_{4,v},a_{4,w})\).  No coefficient is replaced by
an independent norm majorant.  The cubic polynomial arithmetic generates
all Bell-coupling terms automatically.  Method-of-steps segments preserve
the pulse release at one and both breakpoint families generated by
\(4\sqrt5\) and \(5\sqrt5\).

## Refinement envelope

Three DOP853 calculations use

\[
 (r_{tol},a_{tol},h_{max})=
 (2\cdot10^{-9},2\cdot10^{-11},0.04),
 (2\cdot10^{-10},2\cdot10^{-12},0.02),
 (2\cdot10^{-11},2\cdot10^{-13},0.01).
\]

They are sampled on the common two-origin mesh with spacing
\(\sqrt5/24\), 1153 points and 1152 cells through \(24\sqrt5\).  At each
mesh point and in each of the ten coefficient components, the artifact stores
a digest of the finest guide and the pointwise min/max hull of the three
refinements.  The medium-to-fine joint maximum relative difference is about
\(1.59\times10^{-9}\).  The joint absolute difference is about
\(3.09\times10^{-2}\), occurring in a fourth derivative of size about
\(10^{10}\); it must not be interpreted without the parameter scaling.

This min/max hull is called an *observed refinement envelope*.  It contains
the three computed binary64 rows, not the exact RFDE coefficient functions.
It has no directed-rounding or truncation-error status.

## The correlation signal

The finest run observes the following maximum derivative-jet magnitudes in
the voltage component:

\[
 \max|z_1|\approx 9.79\times10^1,
 \quad \max|z_2|\approx3.59\times10^4,
 \quad \max|z_3|\approx1.94\times10^7,
 \quad \max|z_4|\approx1.43\times10^{10}.
\]

Those raw values look large, but at the full wide half-width
\(h=3/40000=7.5\times10^{-5}\), the correlated voltage terms satisfy

\[
 \max|a_1|h\approx7.3422\times10^{-3},\qquad
 \max|a_2|h^2\approx1.0094\times10^{-4},
\]

\[
 \max|a_3|h^3\approx1.3674\times10^{-6},\qquad
 \max|a_4|h^4\approx1.8879\times10^{-8}.
\]

This descending hierarchy is precisely the correlation discarded by the old
zero-centered majorants.

As a further diagnostic, the fourth-order center polynomial is evaluated at
both endpoints \(J_0\pm h\) and compared on the entire mesh with two direct
fine-tolerance physical-pulse flows.  The maximum joint differences are
approximately

\[
 2.63\times10^{-10}\quad\text{and}\quad2.70\times10^{-10}.
\]

This is strong feasibility evidence for a small fifth-order remainder.  It
is not such a bound: both sides are binary64 numerical solutions, their
errors are correlated, and pointwise mesh agreement does not control the
continuous-time supremum.

## What remains for the proof

A rigorous Stage-5A certificate must replace the observed envelope by
directed coefficient-guide errors for all five orders and close the
cellwise \(R_5\) inequality from the Stage-5 contract.  Only after that may
one construct an implicit Route-C event jet and pull the complete history to
the common event graph.  The present artifact leaves the following fields
`null`: directed coefficient radii, order-five remainder, event bracket and
speed, event-time jet, complete-history radius, stable coordinate, and
interval-Newton image.  All event, stable-sheet, onset, and routing flags
remain false.
