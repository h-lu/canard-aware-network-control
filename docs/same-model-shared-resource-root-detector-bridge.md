# A controller-mediated root-to-detector transduction in one shared-resource RFDE

Status: **proved as an exact policy-composition theorem for every finite
network satisfying the hypotheses of the shared-resource selected-root
theorem.** For each fixed \((N,\delta,\zeta)\), the selected complete-history
root and the controlled voltage detector use the same underlying RFDE, the
same scalar recovery coordinate, and the same values of all uncontrolled
model parameters, in particular the same unfolding value
\(a=a_{c,N}(\delta,\zeta)\). The
detector response is deliberately injected by a reset policy that takes the
exact selected roots and the exact model as offline data. Its coefficient and
dimension-uniform remainder are proved below.

This theorem does not identify the selected root with the detector boundary
and does not produce an input-policy-independent relation between them.
The detector is reached under exact-model additive feedback, whereas the
selected root is an intersection of canonical local complete-history traces
of the uncontrolled RFDE. No physical outer selection, unforced onset,
autonomous biological pulse, biological basin, or no-return statement is
proved.

The analytic parent is
[the heterogeneous-curvature selected-root theorem](paper-ii-heterogeneous-curvature-selected-root.md).
The new exact algebra is implemented in
[shared_resource_root_detector_bridge.py](../src/canard_control/shared_resource_root_detector_bridge.py),
generated by
[shared_resource_root_detector_bridge.py](../experiments/shared_resource_root_detector_bridge.py),
and tested in
[test_shared_resource_root_detector_bridge.py](../tests/test_shared_resource_root_detector_bridge.py).

## 1. One RFDE and one recovery architecture

Fix a uniformly admissible family from Theorem 4.1 of the analytic parent.
Thus \(N\ge2\), \(P_N\) is a nonnegative row-stochastic matrix with positive
stationary vector \(\pi_N\), the Dobrushin gap and all operator bounds are
uniform in \(N\), and

\[
 0<c_-\le c_{i,N}\le c_+,
 \qquad \pi_N^Tc_N=\alpha>0.
\tag{1.1}
\]

There is one scalar recovery resource \(w\). For \(\varepsilon=\delta^2\),
write the uncontrolled voltage field as

\[
\begin{aligned}
 F^v_{N,\delta,\zeta}(v_t,w)={}&
 \frac23\mathbf1-w\mathbf1
 -c_N\circ(v(t)-\mathbf1)^{\circ2}
 -\frac\beta3(v(t)-\mathbf1)^{\circ3}
 +D(P_N-I)v(t)\\
 &+\delta^2K\left[
 C_N(\zeta)v(t)-\sum_{k=0}^mB_{k,N}(\zeta)
 v\left(t-\frac{\theta_k}{\delta}\right)
 \right],
\end{aligned}
\tag{1.2}
\]

where

\[
 B_{k,N}(\zeta)=B_{k,N}+\zeta R_{k,N},
 \qquad C_N(\zeta)=\sum_{k=0}^mB_{k,N}(\zeta).
\tag{1.3}
\]

Let \(\mathcal P=(\mathcal P_N)\) be a fixed uniformly admissible
preparation rule for the local graph construction. The parent theorem gives
exactly one preparation-indexed root in its stated local root window,

\[
 \nu_{c,N,\mathcal P_N}(\delta,\zeta),
 \qquad
 \mu_{c,N,\mathcal P_N}(\delta,\zeta)
 =\delta^2\nu_{c,N,\mathcal P_N}(\delta,\zeta),
\tag{1.4}
\]

and

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \Delta_\zeta\mu_{c,N}
 &:=
 \mu_{c,N,\mathcal P_N}(\delta,\zeta)
 -\mu_{c,N,\mathcal P_N}(\delta,0)\\
 &=\mathscr C_N\delta^3\zeta
 +O(\delta^4|\zeta|+\delta^3\zeta^2),
\end{aligned}
\tag{1.5}
\]

with one remainder constant for all admitted \(N\). Here

\[
 \mathscr C_N=-\frac{K}{2\alpha^2}
 \pi_N^T\operatorname{diag}(c_N)
 A_N^{-1}P_{\perp,N}\dot M_{1,N}\mathbf1,
 \qquad \inf_N|\mathscr C_N|>0.
\tag{1.6}
\]

At the selected root set

\[
 a_{c,N}(\delta,\zeta)=1+\mu_{c,N,\mathcal P_N}(\delta,\zeta).
\tag{1.7}
\]

The controlled extension used below is exactly

\[
 \dot v=F^v_{N,\delta,\zeta}(v_t,w)+u^v,
 \qquad
 \dot w=\delta^2(\pi_N^Tv-a_{c,N})+u^w.
\tag{1.8}
\]

For each fixed \((N,\delta,\zeta)\), setting \(u^v=u^w=0\) in (1.8) gives
exactly the shared-resource RFDE of the parent theorem at
\(a=a_{c,N}(\delta,\zeta)\). The controlled stages add inputs to that same
underlying drift; they are not additional uncontrolled stages. There is no
replacement of the scalar \(w\) by an \(N\)-vector of recovery variables.

## 2. Root-linked complete-history preparation

Choose constants

\[
 0<\rho_-<\rho_*<\rho_+<q<\frac{3c_-}{\beta},
 \qquad \lambda\ne0.
\tag{2.1}
\]

Define the reset depth by the exact selected roots,

\[
 \boxed{
 \rho_{N,\mathcal P_N}(\delta,\zeta)
 =\rho_*+\lambda\Delta_\zeta\mu_{c,N}.}
\tag{2.2}
\]

By the uniform estimate (1.5), after decreasing the common
\(\delta_0,\zeta_0\) if necessary, (2.2) lies in
\([\rho_-,\rho_+]\) for every admitted \(N\). The target complete history is

\[
 \Phi_{N,\delta,\zeta}(\theta)
 =\left((1-\rho_{N,\mathcal P_N})\mathbf1,\frac23\right),
 \qquad -\frac{\theta_m}{\delta}\le\theta\le0.
\tag{2.3}
\]

Suppose the initial data lie in the declared bounded cylinder

\[
 \|\phi_v\|_\infty\le V_0,
 \qquad |w(0)|\le W_0.
\tag{2.4}
\]

This cylinder is not asserted to be compact in the RFDE history topology.
With

\[
 \sigma_{1/2}(z)=\operatorname{sgn}(z)\sqrt{|z|},
\tag{2.5}
\]

applied componentwise to vectors, use

\[
\begin{aligned}
 u^v_{\mathrm{pre}}
 &=-F^v_{N,\delta,\zeta}(v_t,w)
 -K_v\sigma_{1/2}
 \left(v-(1-\rho_{N,\mathcal P_N})\mathbf1\right),\\
 u^w_{\mathrm{pre}}
 &=-\delta^2(\pi_N^Tv-a_{c,N})
 -K_w\sigma_{1/2}\left(w-\frac23\right),
\end{aligned}
\tag{2.6}
\]

where \(K_v,K_w>0\). Exact cancellation gives one copy of

\[
 \dot z=-K\operatorname{sgn}(z)\sqrt{|z|}
\tag{2.7}
\]

for every voltage error and for the scalar recovery error. This continuous
field is one-sided Lipschitz and has the forward-unique solution

\[
 z(t)=\operatorname{sgn}(z_0)
 \left(\max\left\{\sqrt{|z_0|}-\frac K2t,0\right\}\right)^2.
\tag{2.8}
\]

Consequently the current state reaches the target no later than the common
bound

\[
 \overline T_{\mathrm{set}}
 :=\max\left\{
 \frac{2\sqrt{V_0+1+\rho_+}}{K_v},
 \frac{2\sqrt{W_0+2/3}}{K_w}
 \right\}.
\tag{2.9}
\]

Keep (2.6) active for one maximum physical delay after that common bound. At

\[
 T_{\mathrm{rel}}=\overline T_{\mathrm{set}}
 +\frac{\theta_m}{\delta},
\tag{2.10}
\]

the complete \((v,w)\)-history is exactly (2.3). The state is continuous and
no overwrite or impulse is used. The
hold time grows like \(\delta^{-1}\); the theorem does not claim a
\(\delta\)-uniform preparation time.

After the exact roots and model coefficients have been supplied offline,
(2.6) is causal as a functional of the current state and the observed delayed
voltage history. No online root estimator, parameter estimator, or adaptive
implementation is part of the theorem.

The input is uniformly bounded in \(N\) on (2.4). To make this explicit,
put

\[
 \mathfrak B
 =\sup_{N,|\zeta|\le\zeta_0}
 \sum_{k=0}^m
 \|B_{k,N}(\zeta)\|_{\mathcal L(\mathbb R^N,\|\cdot\|_N)}<\infty.
\tag{2.11}
\]

During preparation, current states remain between their initial and target
values. If \(V=\max\{V_0,1+\rho_+\}\) and
\(W=\max\{W_0,2/3\}\), then one coarse componentwise authority bound is

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \|u^v_{\mathrm{pre}}\|_\infty
 \le{}&\frac23+W+c_+(V+1)^2+\frac\beta3(V+1)^3
 +2DV+6\delta^2|K|\mathfrak BV\\
 &+K_v\sqrt{V_0+1+\rho_+},\\
 |u^w_{\mathrm{pre}}|
 \le{}&\delta^2(V+1+|\mu_{c,N}|)
 +K_w\sqrt{W_0+2/3}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2.12}
\]

The constants are independent of node count. They depend on the declared
bounded cylinder and the common model box, as they must.

## 3. Exact shared-resource detector dynamics

At release, switch to

\[
\begin{aligned}
 u^v_{\mathrm{det}}
 ={}&-D(P_N-I)v
 -\delta^2K\left[
 C_N(\zeta)v(t)-\sum_{k=0}^mB_{k,N}(\zeta)
 v\left(t-\frac{\theta_k}{\delta}\right)
 \right],\\
 u^w_{\mathrm{det}}
 ={}&-\delta^2(\pi_N^Tv-a_{c,N}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.1}
\]

The second line is one scalar input acting on the same one scalar recovery
resource as in the root theorem. Since \(w(T_{\mathrm{rel}})=2/3\), it gives

\[
 w(t)\equiv\frac23.
\tag{3.2}
\]

Set \(x_i=v_i-1\) and \(s_i=-x_i\). The first line of (3.1) cancels only
the network and delayed feedback. The local fold nonlinearity remains, and
the exact event dynamics are

\[
 \dot x_i=-c_{i,N}x_i^2-\frac\beta3x_i^3,
 \qquad
 \dot s_i=s_i^2\left(c_{i,N}-\frac\beta3s_i\right),
 \qquad s_i(0)=\rho_{N,\mathcal P_N}.
\tag{3.3}
\]

Condition (2.1) makes every \(s_i\) strictly increasing until it reaches
\(q\). Thus some voltage component reaches the detector face

\[
 \min_i(v_i-1)=-q
\tag{3.4}
\]

in finite time. For a node of curvature \(c\), separation of variables
gives the exact latency

\[
 \mathcal T(c;\rho,q)
 =\int_\rho^q\frac{ds}{s^2(c-\beta s/3)}.
\tag{3.5}
\]

Equivalently, with \(d=\beta/3\),

\[
 \mathcal T(c;\rho,q)
 =\frac1c\left(\frac1\rho-\frac1q\right)
 +\frac{d}{c^2}
 \log\frac{q(c-d\rho)}{\rho(c-dq)}.
\tag{3.6}
\]

The integrand is strictly decreasing in \(c\). Hence, writing

\[
 c_{\max,N}=\max_{1\le i\le N}c_{i,N},
\tag{3.7}
\]

the first-hit latency is exactly

\[
 \boxed{
 T_{\mathrm{det},N}(\delta,\zeta)
 =\mathcal T\left(c_{\max,N};
 \rho_{N,\mathcal P_N}(\delta,\zeta),q\right).}
\tag{3.8}
\]

Ties in \(c_{\max,N}\) merely give simultaneous first hits. They do not
alter (3.8). Moreover,

\[
 T_{\mathrm{det},N}
 \le
 \frac{\rho_-^{-1}-q^{-1}}{c_- -\beta q/3},
\tag{3.9}
\]

uniformly in \(N\).

The event-stage controls are bounded on the complete tube before the first
hit. Since present and delayed translated voltages lie in \([-q,0]^N\),

\[
 \|u^v_{\mathrm{det}}\|_\infty
 \le4Dq+4\delta^2|K|\mathfrak Bq,
 \qquad
 |u^w_{\mathrm{det}}|
 \le\delta^2(q+|\mu_{c,N}|).
\tag{3.10}
\]

These are mathematical exact-model bounds, not uncertainty, bandwidth, or
hardware bounds.

## 4. The root-to-latency coefficient

Differentiate (3.5) at its lower endpoint:

\[
 \partial_\rho\mathcal T(c;\rho,q)
 =-\frac{1}{\rho^2(c-\beta\rho/3)}.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Combining (1.5), (2.2), and a uniform second-order Taylor estimate for
\(\mathcal T\) on
\([c_-,c_+]\times[\rho_-,\rho_+]\) gives

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
 &T_{\mathrm{det},N}(\delta,\zeta)
 -T_{\mathrm{det},N}(\delta,0)\\
 &\quad=\mathscr L_N\delta^3\zeta
 +O(\delta^4|\zeta|+\delta^3\zeta^2),\\
 &\mathscr L_N
 =-\frac{\lambda\mathscr C_N}
 {\rho_*^2(c_{\max,N}-\beta\rho_*/3)}.
\end{aligned}}
\tag{4.2}
\]

The remainder constant is independent of \(N\). The quadratic Taylor term
is \(O((\Delta_\zeta\mu_c)^2)=O(\delta^6\zeta^2)\), which is absorbed by
the displayed remainder after \(\delta_0\le1\). Since
\(\lambda\ne0\), \(\inf_N|\mathscr C_N|>0\), and (2.1) holds,

\[
 \inf_N|\mathscr L_N|>0.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Formula (4.2) is an exact controller-mediated composition identity. The reset
policy deliberately encodes the topology-sensitive complete-history root
displacement into a controlled voltage-detector latency without changing the
underlying RFDE or its recovery architecture. It is not dynamical transport
of that displacement along one uncontrolled trajectory.

## 5. Main theorem

> **Theorem 5.1 (one-RFDE controller-mediated root-to-detector transduction).**
> Fix a uniformly admissible shared-resource family and preparation rule
> satisfying Theorem 4.1 of the heterogeneous-curvature selected-root
> theorem, including \(\inf_N|\mathscr C_N|>0\). Fix (2.1) and a bounded
> initial-data cylinder (2.4). Then there are common
> \(\delta_0,\zeta_0>0\) such that, for every \(N\ge2\),
> \(0<\delta<\delta_0\), and \(|\zeta|<\zeta_0\):
>
> 1. the uncontrolled RFDE (1.2), (1.8) with \(u^v=u^w=0\) has exactly one
>    selected complete-history root (1.4) in the local root window of the
>    parent theorem, and that root has the response (1.5);
> 2. the bounded causal additive input (2.6) produces the exact root-linked
>    complete history (2.3) by (2.10), without overwrite or impulse;
> 3. the bounded event input (3.1), using the same scalar recovery
>    coordinate, reaches the detector (3.4) in the exact time (3.8), bounded
>    by (3.9);
> 4. the detector latency has the dimension-uniform, nonzero response
>    (4.2).

**Proof.** Item 1 is the pinned parent theorem. Equations (2.6)--(2.10) and
forward uniqueness of (2.7) prove item 2; the uniform operator bounds give
(2.12). Equations (3.1)--(3.3) are exact algebraic cancellations in the same
RFDE. Separation of (3.3), monotonicity in curvature, and the lower growth
bound

\[
 \dot s_i\ge(c_--\beta q/3)s_i^2
\]

prove item 3 and (3.9). Finally, (4.1), the root estimate (1.5), and Taylor's
theorem prove (4.2), including the uniform remainder and nondegeneracy.
\(\square\)

Theorem 5.1 is a two-stage controlled construction over one underlying RFDE.
It is not an input-independent connection theorem and does not claim that a
single uncontrolled trajectory follows the selected local canard and then
reaches (3.4). Its feedback presupposes offline knowledge of the exact
preparation-indexed roots in (2.2) and of the exact model coefficients; no
root estimator, parameter estimator, or adaptive implementation is proved.

## 6. Explicit all-size no-quotient witness

Use the normalized family from Section 6 of the parent theorem with

\[
 \sigma=\frac15,
 \quad b=2,
 \quad(\theta_0,\theta_1)=(1,4),
 \quad D=3,
 \quad K=1,
 \quad\beta=1.
\tag{6.1}
\]

For every \(N\ge2\), its curvature entries are distinct,

\[
 c_{\max,N}
 =1+\frac15\sqrt{\frac{3(N-1)}{N+1}},
 \qquad
 c_{i,N}>1-\frac{\sqrt3}{5},
\tag{6.2}
\]

it has no nontrivial synchrony quotient, and

\[
 \mathscr C_N=-\frac1{10}.
\tag{6.3}
\]

Take

\[
 \rho_*=\frac14,
 \quad [\rho_-,\rho_+]=\left[\frac15,\frac3{10}\right],
 \quad q=\frac12,
 \quad\lambda=1.
\tag{6.4}
\]

Then

\[
 c_- -\frac q3
 =\frac56-\frac{\sqrt3}{5}>0,
\tag{6.5}
\]

and the bridge coefficient is

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathscr L_N
 =\frac{1}{10(1/4)^2(c_{\max,N}-1/12)}>0.}
\tag{6.6}
\]

Thus this nonzero controller-mediated coefficient is realized for every
finite node count and has one sign for the whole family.

## 7. Why the previous two theorem branches did not imply this result

The shared-resource root theorem and the balanced FHN control theorem cannot
be composed merely because both have FHN-type voltage variables. Their phase
spaces have different recovery dimensions. In the nodewise model, equality
of all recovery coordinates is not invariant along a nonsynchronous voltage
state: if \(w_i=w_j\) but \(v_i\ne v_j\), then already

\[
 \dot w_i-\dot w_j=\varepsilon(v_i-v_j)
\tag{7.1}
\]

when the diffusive recovery contribution vanishes. Hence the shared scalar
resource is not a restriction of the nodewise recovery model on the
nonsynchronous histories needed by the network-root mechanism. Conversely,
projecting nodewise recovery onto \(\pi^Tw\) discards transverse recovery
coordinates and does not reproduce the nodewise controlled system.

Theorem 5.1 removes this phase-space mismatch for the stated controlled
composition: both objects now live in (1.8). It does not repair the
local-to-global physical-selection gap.

There is also a direct hostile test against overinterpreting (4.2). Replace
the policy (2.2) by

\[
 \rho=\rho_*+\lambda\Delta_\zeta\mu_c+\eta\delta^3\zeta.
\tag{7.2}
\]

This leaves the uncontrolled RFDE and its selected canard root unchanged,
but it changes the leading detector-latency coefficient by

\[
 -\frac{\eta}{\rho_*^2(c_{\max,N}-\beta\rho_*/3)}.
\tag{7.3}
\]

Therefore the controlled latency is policy dependent while the uncontrolled
root is unchanged. Theorem 5.1 alone cannot identify its latency with an
intrinsic physical onset; at most a separate comparison theorem could show
that one specially chosen policy reproduces such an onset.

## 8. The minimum still-missing physical comparison theorem

An intrinsic canard-to-pulse theorem would need at least the following new
lemma, none of whose clauses is supplied by exact cancellation:

> **Open comparison lemma.** Construct parameter-coherent attracting and
> repelling outer RFDE histories for the uncontrolled shared-resource model,
> prove that their entries into the uncut fold graph select the same traces
> used in (1.4), construct an uncontrolled pulse/quiet separator functional
> \(E_N(\mu,\zeta)\), and prove on a common neighborhood either
> \(E_N=\chi_ND_N\) with \(\inf|\chi_N|>0\), or another uniform equivalence
> of their simple zero sets. Then prove that the two signs of \(E_N\) enter
> distinct global channels, one with a certified voltage excursion and a
> no-return property.

Until that lemma is proved, the implications

\[
 \text{selected complete-history root}
 \Longrightarrow \text{unforced onset}
 \Longrightarrow \text{autonomous biological pulse}
\tag{8.1}
\]

remain open.
