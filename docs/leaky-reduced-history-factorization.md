## 1. Projection and compatible lift

Put \(r=5\sqrt5\) and define

\[
 X=C([-r,0],\mathbb R^2),\qquad
 Y=C([-r,0],\mathbb R)\times\mathbb R.
\]

The continuous projection

\[
 \pi(\phi_v,\phi_w)=(\phi_v,\phi_w(0))
\tag{1.1}
\]

retains exactly the data read by the future vector field.  If
\(y=(q,\omega)\in Y\), define

\[
 \iota(q,\omega)=(q,\mathcal R(q,\omega)),
\]

where

\[
 \mathcal R(q,\omega)(\theta)
 =e^{-\varepsilon\theta}
 \left[\omega-\varepsilon\int_\theta^0
 e^{\varepsilon s}(q(s)-a)\,ds\right].
\tag{1.2}
\]

Direct differentiation gives

\[
 \mathcal R'=\varepsilon(q-a-\mathcal R),
 \qquad \mathcal R(0)=\omega,
\tag{1.3}
\]

so \(\pi\iota=I_Y\).  The derivative of the lift is

\[
 D\mathcal R(q,\omega)[h,\eta](\theta)
 =e^{-\varepsilon\theta}
 \left[\eta-\varepsilon\int_\theta^0e^{\varepsilon s}h(s)\,ds\right].
\tag{1.4}
\]

Thus \(D\iota\) is a continuous linear right inverse of \(\pi\).
Here and below the state derivative is taken at fixed \(a\).  A later
parameter derivative with respect to \(a\) must also differentiate the
lift (1.2); no such parameter-response formula is asserted here.

## 2. Exact semiflow factorization

Let \(\Phi_t\) be the semiflow on \(X\).  Two initial histories with the
same image under \(\pi\) have the same voltage history and current recovery
value.  Uniqueness therefore gives the same future solution.  Consequently
the definition \(\Psi_t=\pi\Phi_t\iota\) is independent of the chosen full
lift and defines a semiflow on \(Y\) satisfying

\[
 \pi\Phi_t=\Psi_t\pi,qquad t\ge0.
\tag{2.1}
\]

After one maximum delay, every retained recovery value has been generated by
the current-state recovery ODE.  Equation (1.2) then reconstructs the entire
recovery history, and

\[
 \Phi_t=\iota\Psi_t\pi,qquad t\ge r.
\tag{2.2}
\]

This is an exact factorization, not an inertial-manifold approximation.

\[

\tag{3.1}
\]
