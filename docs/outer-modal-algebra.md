# Physical outer modal algebra

Status: **exact physical equations and exact formal Taylor jets.** This note
does not prove the parameter-coherent outer slow-history selection needed by
physical outer-selection Gate D. It records the algebra on which that proof
must be based.

Let

\[
 v-v_*=r\xi+q\zeta,
 \qquad
 w-w_*=r\rho+q\kappa,
 \qquad \varepsilon=\delta^2.
\]

Undoing the anisotropic inner scaling in the exact chart gives

\[
 \dot\rho=\varepsilon(\xi-\mu),
 \qquad
 \dot\kappa=\varepsilon\zeta-D_w\kappa.
\tag{1}
\]

The two voltage equations are exact cubic polynomials plus the weak history
terms

\[
 \varepsilon K\left(
  \xi-\frac13\xi_0-\frac23\xi_1
  -\frac16\zeta+\frac1{12}\zeta_0+\frac1{12}\zeta_1
 \right)
\tag{2}
\]

in the critical equation and

\[
 \varepsilon K\left[
  \frac16\zeta-\frac1{12}\zeta_0-\frac1{12}\zeta_1
  +\eta(-\xi_0+\xi_1-\zeta_0+\zeta_1)
 \right]
\tag{3}
\]

in the transverse equation.  The executable audit retains every local
polynomial term rather than replacing it by big-O notation.

In slow time \(T=\varepsilon t\), the physical delay is small exactly:

\[
 \varepsilon\frac{\theta_k}{\delta}=\delta\theta_k.
\tag{4}
\]

Thus ambient backward propagation in fast time is nonuniform, but a
curve-restricted outer parameterization sees a shift of order \(\delta\).
Equation (4) is an algebraic fact; a tame curve-wise delay map still has to
be constructed.

At \(\varepsilon=0\), \(\kappa=0\), the local fast-equilibrium branch has

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \zeta_0(\xi)
  &=-\frac{\alpha}{2}\xi^2+\frac7{16}\xi^3
    +\frac{9\sqrt6}{256}\xi^4+O(\xi^5),\\
 \rho_0(\xi)
  &=-\alpha\xi^2-\frac{11}{24}\xi^3
    -\frac{55\sqrt6}{128}\xi^4+O(\xi^5).
\end{aligned}
\tag{5}
\]

The small fast eigenvalue along this curve is

\[
 \lambda_c(\xi)
 =-2\alpha\xi-\frac{11}{8}\xi^2
   -\frac{49\sqrt6}{32}\xi^3+O(\xi^4).
\tag{6}
\]

Hence \(\xi>0\) is the attracting local branch and \(\xi<0\) is the
repelling local branch, consistently with the corrected transition sections
in `long-delay-selected-trace-proof.md`.  At the logarithmic matching radius
\(|\xi|\asymp\delta S_\delta\), the normal gap is of order
\(\delta S_\delta\), whereas the history forcing retains the

mixed parameter bounds is the remaining outer selection theorem, not a
