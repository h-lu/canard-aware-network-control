# The fixed-\(\varepsilon\) complete-history BVP for the quadratic root

Status: **exact BVP and adjoint validation contract, with a binary64
finite-section shooting diagnostic only.**  At \(\varepsilon=1/5\), three
forward shooting sections give negative response candidates between
\(-0.350\) and \(-0.264\), with central value

\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{shoot}}\approx-0.2809514461.
\tag{0.1}
\]

This is consistent in sign with the small-\(\delta\) predictor
\(-T_*/50\approx-0.3308077560\), but the section spread is about \(0.086\).
The shooting calculation does not construct the selected repelling trace,
solve a complete-history jump, validate an augmented inverse, or compute the
dynamic adjoint.  It is not a proof that a fixed-\(\varepsilon\) selected root
exists or that \(\rho_*\ne0\).

**Function-space correction.**  The endpoint geometry below remains the
target, but the original \(W^{2,p}\to L^p\) branch contract has been replaced
by the proved
[sliding-window/\(W^{1,p}\) bridge](fixed-epsilon-sliding-window-w1p-bridge.md).
For fixed delays and flight times, the principal orbit scale is
\(W^{1,p}\to L^p\), with full history residuals in \(W^{1,p}\).
Continuous piecewise polynomials are conforming on this scale; endpoint
derivative compatibility is inherited from complete selected orbits rather
than imposed as a continuous \(W^{1,p}\) row.  The old
\(W^{2,p}\to L^p\) branch columns are compact and cannot form the desired
Fredholm operator.

## 1. Fixed plant, chart, and history horizon

On the synchronous leaf, the physical RFDE is

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \dot V={}&V-\frac{V^3}{3}-W\\
 &+\varepsilon\kappa_1
 \left\{\frac{V(t-\tau_0)+V(t-\tau_1)}2-V\right\}\\
 &+\varepsilon\kappa_3
 \left\{\frac{(V(t-\tau_0)-1)^3+(V(t-\tau_1)-1)^3}{2}
 -(V-1)^3\right\}\\
 &+\varepsilon\eta
 \left\{(V-1)^2-(V(t-T_*)-1)^2\right\},\\
 \dot W={}&\varepsilon(V-a),
\end{aligned}
\tag{1.1}
\]

with

\[
 \varepsilon=\frac15,\qquad
 (\kappa_1,\kappa_3)=\left(\frac15,\frac14\right),\qquad
 (\tau_0,\tau_1)=(4\sqrt5,5\sqrt5).
\tag{1.2}
\]

Here \(T_*\) is the exact center-periodic-orbit period and is a fixed delay
of the plant.  Since its validated numerical value is near \(16.5404\),

\[
 h=\max\{4\sqrt5,5\sqrt5,T_*\}=T_*.
\tag{1.3}
\]

The RFDE phase space is \(C([-h,0],\mathbb R^2)\), and classical histories
belong to its solution manifold.  The extra interval
\([-T_*,-5\sqrt5]\) must be present even at \(\eta=0\): although the drift
then ignores it, its derivative in \(\eta\) reads it.

Put \(\delta=1/\sqrt5\), \(s=\delta t\), and

\[
 V=1+\delta X,\qquad
 W=\frac23-\delta^2Y,\qquad
 a=1+\delta^2\nu,\qquad
 \Theta_*=\delta T_*.
\tag{1.4}
\]

The exact fold-chart RFDE is

\[
\begin{aligned}
 X'={}&Y-X^2\\
 &+\delta\left[-\frac{X^3}{3}
 +\kappa_1\left\{\frac{X(s-4)+X(s-5)}2-X\right\}\right]\\
 &+\delta^2\eta\{X^2-X(s-\Theta_*)^2\}\\
 &+\delta^3\kappa_3
 \left\{\frac{X(s-4)^3+X(s-5)^3}{2}-X^3\right\},\\
 Y'={}&-X+\delta\nu.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1.5}
\]

No history Taylor expansion is used in (1.5).

## 2. Smallest faithful two-sided BVP

Fix finite left and right flight lengths \(L_-,L_+>0\).  A selected trace
theorem must provide:

- an attracting entry bundle
  \(\mathcal B_-(x^-_{-L_-},\xi_-;a,\eta)=0\) on the full horizon \(h\);
- a backward-extendible repelling exit bundle
  \(\mathcal B_+(x^+_{L_+},\xi_+;a,\eta)=0\);
- a parameter-coherent choice of both bundles and their first two parameter
  derivatives.

The unknown is

\[
 z=(x^-,x^+,\xi_-,\xi_+),
\tag{2.1}
\]

where

\[
\begin{aligned}
 x^-&\in W^{1,p}([-L_--h,0],\mathbb R^2),\\
 x^+&\in W^{1,p}([-h,L_+],\mathbb R^2).
\end{aligned}
\tag{2.2}
\]

All entry, seam, and exit histories are compared in
\(H=W^{1,p}([-h,0],\mathbb R^2)\).  Exact zeros regularize to classical
flights a posteriori.  If \(T_*\) or \(L_\pm\) is varied, a Banach-scale or
stronger-space column is required for the corresponding moving translation.

Let \(\mathcal F\) denote the right-hand side of (1.1).  The full residual is

\[
\mathfrak F(z,a,\eta)=
\begin{pmatrix}
 \dot x^-(t)-\mathcal F(x^-_t;a,\eta)\\
 \dot x^+(t)-\mathcal F(x^+_t;a,\eta)\\
 \mathcal B_-(x^-_{-L_-},\xi_-;a,\eta)\\
 \mathcal B_+(x^+_{L_+},\xi_+;a,\eta)\\
 V^-(0)-1\\
 J
\end{pmatrix},
\tag{2.3}
\]

where the phase is \(V^-(0)=1\), and the jump is the entire history

\[
 J(\theta)=x^-(\theta)-x^+(\theta),
 \qquad -h\le\theta\le0.
\tag{2.4}
\]

Matching only \(x^-(0)=x^+(0)\), or only the voltage observable, is not an
RFDE connection.  After the phase is imposed, require
\(D_z\mathfrak F\) to have a one-dimensional cokernel.  Choose a complement
\(e\) supported in the jump slot and validate the isomorphism

\[
 \widehat L(\dot z,\dot d)
 =D_z\mathfrak F\,\dot z-\dot d\,e.
\tag{2.5}
\]

Solving

\[
 \mathfrak F(z(a,\eta),a,\eta)=d(a,\eta)e
\tag{2.6}
\]

defines the strict Lin gap.  The desired root is \(d=0\).  Its interpretation
as equality of selected slow histories additionally requires the endpoint
zero-fiber implication; (2.4) alone proves only a matched complete-history
connection for the selected fibers.

## 3. Linearization and dynamic adjoint

In fold time, write the variational equation at \(\eta=0\) as

\[
 u'(s)=A_0(s)u(s)+A_4(s)u(s-4)+A_5(s)u(s-5).
\tag{3.1}
\]

The quadratic \(T_*\)-channel has zero state-linearization coefficient at
\(\eta=0\), so there is no \(A_{\Theta_*}\) term in (3.1).  The exact
nonzero entries are

\[
\begin{aligned}
 (A_0)_{11}
 &=-2X+\delta(-X^2-\kappa_1)-3\delta^3\kappa_3X^2,
 &(A_0)_{12}&=1,\\
 (A_0)_{21}&=-1,
 &(A_4)_{11}
 &=\frac{\delta\kappa_1}{2}
 +\frac{3\delta^3\kappa_3}{2}X(s-4)^2,\\
 (A_5)_{11}
 &=\frac{\delta\kappa_1}{2}
 +\frac{3\delta^3\kappa_3}{2}X(s-5)^2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.2}
\]

On an interior interval \(I\), the dynamic adjoint is advanced:

\[
 -p'(s)=A_0(s)^Tp(s)
 +\sum_{\theta\in\{4,5\}}
 \mathbf1_{\{s+\theta\in I\}}
 A_\theta(s+\theta)^Tp(s+\theta).
\tag{3.3}
\]

Equation (3.3) is only its differential part.  The full cokernel covector
\(\Psi\) also contains entry, exit, phase, seam, and complete-jump
multipliers.  Normalize it by

\[
 \Psi D_z\mathfrak F=0,\qquad \langle\Psi,e\rangle=1.
\tag{3.4}
\]

With residual convention \(\dot x-\mathcal F\), the physical interior
columns are

\[
 \mathfrak F_a^{\mathrm{int}}
 =\begin{pmatrix}0\\ \varepsilon\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 \mathfrak F_\eta^{\mathrm{int}}
 =\begin{pmatrix}
 -\varepsilon\{(V-1)^2-(V(t-T_*)-1)^2\}\\
 0
 \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{3.5}
\]

All endpoint derivatives must be appended before pairing.  Define

\[
 m_a=\langle\Psi,\mathfrak F_a\rangle,\qquad
 m_\eta=\langle\Psi,\mathfrak F_\eta\rangle.
\tag{3.6}
\]

The exact fixed-\(\varepsilon\) response is

\[
 \boxed{\rho_*=-\frac{m_\eta}{m_a}.}
\tag{3.7}
\]

Neither the static collective vector nor the small-\(\delta\) Gaussian
adjoint may replace \(\Psi\) in (3.7).

## 4. Required interval certificate

A feasible rigorous implementation should use piecewise Chebyshev
coefficients on a mesh closed under translations by
\(4\sqrt5,5\sqrt5,T_*\).  The unknown includes both older-history pieces in
(2.2), the entry and exit coordinates, \(a\), and the scalar gap.
Polynomial nonlinearities are bounded in a weighted coefficient
\(\ell^1_\nu\) algebra.  Shifted evaluations and differentiation require
separate tail bounds.

For a numerical candidate \(\bar z\) and an approximate inverse \(A\), a
radii-polynomial proof must provide directed bounds

\[
\begin{aligned}
 Y&\ge\|A\mathfrak F(\bar z)\|,\\
 Z_1&\ge\|I-AD\mathfrak F(\bar z)\|,\\
 Z_2(r)&\ge
 \sup_{\|z-\bar z\|\le r}
 \frac{\|A(D\mathfrak F(z)-D\mathfrak F(\bar z))\|}{r},
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.1}
\]

and a positive \(r\) such that

\[
 Y+(Z_1-1)r+Z_2(r)r^2<0.
\tag{4.2}
\]

The same certificate must enclose the normalized adjoint and prove

\[
 0\notin I_a\ni m_a,\qquad
 0\notin I_\eta\ni m_\eta.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Then \(-I_\eta/I_a\) is the rigorous enclosure of \(\rho_*\).  A
second-derivative bound is still required to obtain a nonempty
\(\eta\)-neighborhood rather than one point derivative.

## 5. What the current shooting diagnostic computes

The diagnostic prescribes, for \(s\le-L\), the singular history

\[
 (X_0(s),Y_0(s))
 =\left(-\frac{s}{2},\frac{s^2-2}{4}\right),
\tag{5.1}
\]

integrates (1.5) forward from \(-L\) to \(L\), and tunes \(\nu\) so that

\[
 G_L(\nu,\eta)
 =\frac{X(L)^2}{2}-\frac{Y(L)}{2}-\frac14=0.
\tag{5.2}
\]

The prescribed older history is independent of \((\nu,\eta)\).  Forward
sensitivities give

\[
 \rho_L
 =-\delta^2\frac{\partial_\eta G_L}{\partial_\nu G_L}.
\tag{5.3}
\]

They are checked against centered finite differences in \(\eta\).  The
results are:

| \(L\) | \(\nu_L\) | \(a_L\) | \(\partial_\nu G_L\) | scalar \(1/|\partial_\nu G_L|\) | \(\rho_L\) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2.5 | 0.2404254335 | 1.0480850867 | -1.9755408874 | 0.5061904850 | -0.3496537898 |
| 3.0 | 0.1639457360 | 1.0327891472 | -7.1697479311 | 0.1394749173 | -0.2809514461 |
| 3.5 | 0.1150286320 | 1.0230057264 | -25.9033715730 | 0.0386050131 | -0.2637623358 |

The maximum forward/finite-difference disagreement is about
\(1.3\times10^{-6}\); the maximum root residual is about
\(3.8\times10^{-8}\).  These are binary64 solver diagnostics, not interval
bounds.  The displayed scalar inverse concerns only the one-dimensional
shooting gap.  It is not the norm of the augmented complete-history inverse
(2.5).

More importantly,

\[
 \max_L\rho_L-\min_L\rho_L\approx0.08589,\qquad
 \max_La_L-\min_La_L\approx0.02508.
\tag{5.4}
\]

This section dependence is much larger than the numerical integration error.
It confirms that the forward prescription (5.1) has not selected the
two-sided root in (2.3)--(2.6).

## 6. Feasibility verdict

Rigorous fixed-\(\varepsilon\) validation is not currently executable from
the existing repository infrastructure:

1. the canonical small-\(\delta\) Green theorem gives analytic traces only
   on an unquantified small-\(\delta\) wedge and cannot certify its hypotheses
   at \(1/\sqrt5\);
2. the exact-chart method-of-steps code is a one-sided IVP and has no
   repelling endpoint bundle or complete-history jump;
3. the periodic Fourier/Chebyshev validators use periodic boundary
   conditions and a known phase-bordered index, not the Lin trace pair in
   (2.3);
4. no current interval code represents the two older-history pieces,
   endpoint coordinates, scalar jump complement, and advanced adjoint in one
   coefficient-space operator;
5. no fixed-\(\varepsilon\) outer selection or endpoint zero-fiber theorem is
   available.

The shortest faithful implementation is therefore:

1. construct numerical entry and exit trace bundles by continuation from the
   proved small-\(\delta\) canonical family toward \(\delta=1/\sqrt5\);
2. solve (2.3)--(2.6) in a shift-closed piecewise Chebyshev mesh;
3. implement coefficient-space tail bounds and validate (4.1)--(4.2);
4. validate the transposed bordered system and (4.3);
5. only then promote the fixed-\(\varepsilon\) root and response.
