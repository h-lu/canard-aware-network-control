\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

The shared-resource construction proves a dimension-uniform selected
complete-history root and a nonzero response to delayed-layer redistribution
in a finite retarded network class without a nontrivial synchrony quotient.
The central
mechanism is not synchrony alone: transverse Dobrushin contraction first
supplies a uniform inverse, the invariant-history graph and one-sided traces
solve the range problem, and only then does the mixed-curvature pairing
produce the scalar root displacement.  The companion leaky-network theorem
shows how the same transverse inverse corrects the full cokernel when delayed
layers are row-mass preserving but not left balanced.

The two root statements have different scopes.  In the leaky model, the
scalar phase-fixed root is an input to the canonical transfer theorem.  In
the shared-resource model, the root is constructed, but its exact
finite-\(\delta\) value is selected by the fixed canonical preparation.  The
uniform conclusion concerns existence, uniqueness in the stated local
window, the leading structural coefficient, and the remainder in
\cref{eq:shared-response}; it does not provide a preparation-independent
physical canard or a comparison theorem for arbitrary graph preparations.
Establishing such independence, allowing moving delay support, or allowing a
closing Dobrushin gap requires additional arguments not contained here.
