\section{Notation and logical status}
\label{sec:notation-status}

The paper uses three levels of description: the full RFDE history, the
stationary collective projection, and its transverse complement.  The symbols
used throughout are collected below.

\begin{center}
\small

Symbol & Meaning\\
\midrule
\(N,P_N,\pi_N\) &
Finite network size, row-stochastic Markov matrix, and its strictly positive
stationary probability.\\
\(E_N,A_N\) &
Transverse space \(E_N=\ker\pi_N^T\) and stable Markov generator
\(A_N=D(P_N-\Id)|_{E_N}\).\\
\(\Pi_N,\mathcal F\) &
Stationary projection \(\Pi_N(v,w)=(\pi_N^Tv,w)\) and the full RFDE vector
field on the unreduced history space.\\
\(\Theta,\Delta_\theta\) &
Merged delay support and its diameter
\(\Delta_\theta=\max\Theta-\min\Theta\).\\

Structural delay-layer redistribution in
\(\mathfrak T_N^{\rm red}\), with \(\pi_N^TR_k=0\) and
\(\sum_kR_k=0\).  A ray is written \(\eta=\zeta R\).\\
