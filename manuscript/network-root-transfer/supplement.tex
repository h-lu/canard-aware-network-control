This supplement contains one secondary model realization and the construction
and bookkeeping proofs used in the main article.  The numbering and notation
of the main article are retained in cross-references.  The first appendix
records the heterogeneous recovery-sensing realization.  It is followed by
the preparation and invariant-history arguments, a compressed outer-passage
toolkit, and concise anchored half-line, stable-sheet, and
complete-history connection proofs.
