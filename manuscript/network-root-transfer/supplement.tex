main article.  The numbering and notation of the main article are retained in
cross-references.  Section A gives the preparation and noncanonicity
constructions, Section B the invariant complete-history graph, Section C
the finite-section bridge, and Section D the critical-layer geometry and
positive-\(\delta\) frozen-voltage history splitting, exact
tracker-quotient identities, and the history-selection obstruction.
