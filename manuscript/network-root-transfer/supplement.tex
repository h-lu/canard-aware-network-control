This supplement contains only the proof components needed by the main
article.  The numbering and notation of the main article are retained in
cross-references.  The first appendix supplies the fold scaling, the locally
invariant manifold of compatible histories, and the finite-interval matching function.  The
second records a functional-analytic formulation of the additional global
invariant-manifold and comparison hypotheses used in the conditional
heteroclinic application.  It does not verify those hypotheses for the
displayed global extension.
