\textbf{(a)} At \((\delta,S)=(0.05,3)\), the computed zero

\textbf{(b)} The centered quotient
\(\widehat q_{\delta}
 =\{\widehat\nu_{\rm sec}(\delta,\zeta_*;S)
             -\widehat\nu_{\rm sec}(\delta,-\zeta_*;S)\}
            /(2\zeta_*\delta)\), equivalently the centered difference of
\(\widehat\mu_{\rm sec}=\delta^2\widehat\nu_{\rm sec}\) divided by
\(\delta^3\),

\(\Lambda_3=-2\sigma/3=-1/3\) along
\((\delta,S)=(0.12,2.5),(0.08,2.75),(0.05,3),(0.02,3.5),(0.01,4)\).
When the two opposite perturbation terms are evaluated at the same delayed
state, they cancel while the baseline coupling is retained, and the control
quotient is exactly zero.  Curves and
symbols are numerical; the projection identity, coefficient, and control
cancellation are exact.  This prescribed-history, finite-section calculation
is not \(D_3^{\rm fin}\), does not construct the invariant history graph, and
does not compute a heteroclinic connection or a maximal canard.}
