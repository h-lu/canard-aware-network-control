\caption{How to read the three-node numerical illustration.
\textbf{(a)} The open symbols are the intersections of the three computed
section-mismatch curves
\(\widehat E_S=Y(S)-X(S)^2+1/2\) with the gray zero level.  Their horizontal movement
shows that redistributing the perturbation between distinct delays moves the
finite-section zero, although the stationary projection of the RFDE vector
field is exactly unchanged at every fixed full history.
\textbf{(b)} The blue values are the scaled zero displacement
\(\widehat q_{\delta}
 =\{\widehat\nu_{\rm sec}(\delta,\zeta_*;S)
             -\widehat\nu_{\rm sec}(\delta,-\zeta_*;S)\}
            /(2\zeta_*\delta)\); their proximity to the dashed line shows
agreement in sign and scale with the exact Fredholm coefficient
\(\Lambda_3=-1/3\).  In the green same-delay control, the perturbation terms
cancel and the displacement is exactly zero.
We use \(\sigma=1/2\), \(\beta=3\), \(D=1\), \(K=2\),
\((\theta_0,\theta_1)=(0,1)\), and \(\zeta_*=0.04\).  The exact
fold-coordinate RFDE is integrated from the prescribed singular history by a
method-of-steps Radau solver.  Panel~\textbf{(a)} uses
\((\delta,S)=(0.05,3)\); panel~\textbf{(b)} uses
\((\delta,S)=(0.12,2.5),(0.08,2.75),(0.05,3),(0.02,3.5),(0.01,4)\).
Curves and symbols are numerical; the projection identity, coefficient, and
same-delay cancellation are exact.  This prescribed-history calculation is
not \(D_3^{\rm fin}\) and does not compute a heteroclinic connection or a
maximal canard.}
