# Figure plan: conditional connection and its defining function

- Reader question and result served: How are the projected fold geometry and
  the stable-manifold membership condition in the RFDE phase section related
  in the conditional connection corollary?
- Status: `SCHEMATIC`.  The orientation and local maximum at the fold follow
  from the displayed coordinates and singular orbit, but all placements and
  distances are qualitative.  The figure assumes the hypotheses of the
  conditional corollary; it is not an existence proof.
- System, parameters, coordinates, and time convention: one fixed globally
  modified RFDE.  Panel (a) uses the collective projection `(-r,w)` and arrows
  indicate increasing original time (equivalently increasing fold time).
  Panel (b) is a two-dimensional slice of the Banach-manifold chart
  `(psi,u)` on `M^2_{Sigma_0}` and is shown at a nearby parameter with
  `G != 0`.
- Objects, directions, and representative choices: panel (a) shows the
  frozen critical curve, its attracting and repelling branches, the fold,
  the specified conditional orbit at `G=0`, the outer equilibria `Z_N^+` and
  `Z_N^-`, the agreement region `|r|<=r_loc`, and the common phase section.
  Panel (b) shows the incoming point `A^0=(psi_A,u_A)`, the section of
  `W^s(Z_N^-)` in `M^2_{Sigma_0}` represented by `u=F^-(psi)`, and the
  scalar normal gap `G=u_A-F^-(psi_A)`.
- Panels and reading order: (a) the conditional global projection; (b) the
  defining function on the common section.
- Line, marker, fill, color, and arrow meanings: dashed green denotes the
  frozen critical curve; solid blue denotes the single specified orbit and
  filled blue circles denote its section point; solid orange denotes the
  stable-manifold section in the slice; the double arrow is the signed scalar
  gap; the pale vertical strip is the exact agreement condition in the
  projected present coordinate.  Line style, marker, and direct labels make
  all distinctions independent of color.
- What the figure must not imply: the drawing is not the full RFDE phase
  space; the stable-manifold section is not one-dimensional; the critical
  curve is not a finite-parameter slow manifold; the solid curve is not a
  computed orbit; the displayed gap in panel (b) is generic and collapses at
  a zero; the section point in panel (a) is not meant to coincide exactly
  with an extremum of the finite-parameter orbit; the conditional corollary
  does not prove the required global
  invariant-manifold or comparison hypotheses; endpoint heights and all
  distances are nonquantitative.
- Caption draft: included in `connection-geometry.tex`.
- Production route and editable source: Matplotlib vector PDF generated by
  `connection_geometry.py`.
- Final placement and size: full text width after the conditional
  heteroclinic-connection and sensitivity corollaries.
- Checks needed before delivery: fold is a local maximum; time arrows point
