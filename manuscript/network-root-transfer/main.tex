\begin{abstract}
We study finite networks of retarded functional differential equations with
row-stochastic instantaneous coupling and several delayed-coupling layers.
The perturbations preserve the sum of the layer matrices and are annihilated
by the stationary row at each delay.  Hence the stationary projection of the
vector field is unchanged at every history, although the projected variables
need not form a closed system.  We determine the transverse first variation
entering a codimension-one Fredholm solvability condition near a fast--slow
fold.  With two distinct delays, the first delay moment maps onto the
stationary-mean-zero subspace on the full matrix tangent space defined only by
those linear constraints; sparsity, symmetry, layerwise row sums, and signs
are unrestricted.  We compute its norm and a norm-optimal right inverse.  For
a prescribed linear matrix pattern, the range is the image of
\(R\mapsto R\mathbf1\); a generator-supported pattern has a uniform right
inverse under Dobrushin contraction.  Block elimination gives
\[
 \Lambda_N=\mathfrak r_NA_N^{-1}\mathsf S_N.
\]
For a polynomial fast--slow network, we construct a local invariant manifold
and identify \(\Lambda_N\) in the derivative of a finite-interval matching
function.  A positive-delay computation and an explicit growing family
illustrate a nonzero coefficient uniform in network size.  A final conditional
theorem transfers this coefficient to a heteroclinic parameter if the global
invariant manifolds and a uniform \(C^1\) comparison are constructed
independently; those properties are not verified here.
\end{abstract}
