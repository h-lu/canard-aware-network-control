\begin{abstract}
We study finite retarded networks in which a redistribution of delayed
coupling is exactly invisible to a stationary projected vector-field
functional at every unreduced history.  With two distinct delay locations,
these projection-blind redistributions nevertheless generate every transverse
forcing direction, with sharp right-inverse and dual condition bounds uniform
in network size under a common Dobrushin gap; the dual bound uses the inherited
norm on the observation range.  Near a collective fold, we construct an
invariant RFDE history graph and selected one-sided traces.  For each fixed
admissible preparation and uniformly bounded blind direction family
\(\mathbf R_N\), their nonlinear complete-history matching root satisfies
\[
 \mu_{c,N}^{\mathbf p,\mathbf R_N}(\delta,\zeta)
 -\mu_{c,N}^{\mathbf p,\mathbf R_N}(\delta,0)
 =\delta^3\zeta\Lambda_N(\mathbf R_N)
  +O(\delta^4|\zeta|+\delta^3\zeta^2)
\]
uniformly in network size.  Normalized root increments recover evaluations of
the response covector with error \(O(\delta+|\zeta|)\), and a spanning probe
family reconstructs the compressed transverse return covector.  Generator
probes preserve the chosen base-layer support and, at each fixed \(N\), recover
centered curvature or recovery-sensing profiles from weighted pairings.  When
the merged delay support contains only one location, pure redistributions
generate no transverse source, giving a sharp no-go result for this mechanism.
Fast curvature and heterogeneous recovery sensing give inequivalent
realizations.  Sparse reset--cycle examples have entrywise nonnegative
perturbed layers and rank-\(N-1\) structural directions.  The exact
finite-section root depends on the outer completion; only its
baseline-subtracted leading response germ is canonical within the fixed
projection, matching, and normalization data.
\end{abstract}
