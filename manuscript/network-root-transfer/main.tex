\begin{abstract}
We study finite networks of retarded functional differential equations with
row-stochastic instantaneous and multilayer delayed coupling.  Perturbations
preserve the layer sum and are annihilated by the stationary row at each
delay.  Thus the stationary projection of the vector field is
unchanged at every history, although the projected variables need not form a
closed system.  We determine the transverse first variation entering a
codimension-one Fredholm solvability condition near a fast--slow fold.  With
two distinct delays, the first delay moment maps the full admissible matrix
tangent space onto the stationary-mean-zero subspace and admits a norm-optimal
right inverse.  Under a prescribed matrix pattern, its range is the image of
\(R\mapsto R\mathbf1\); a generator-supported pattern admits a uniform right
inverse under Dobrushin contraction.  Block elimination yields
\(\Lambda_N=\mathfrak r_NA_N^{-1}\mathsf S_N\).  For a polynomial fast--slow
network, we construct a local invariant manifold and identify \(\Lambda_N\)
in the derivative of a finite-interval matching function.  A computation with
two positive delays and a growing network family exhibits a nonzero
coefficient uniform in network size.  A conditional theorem transfers this
coefficient to a heteroclinic parameter if global invariant manifolds and the
required uniform \(C^1\) comparison are independently established; neither is
verified here.
\end{abstract}
