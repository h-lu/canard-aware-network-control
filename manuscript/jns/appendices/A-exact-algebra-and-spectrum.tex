\section{Exact algebra in the prepared fold chart}
\label{app:exact-algebra}

This appendix records the finite-dimensional identities and the exact
rescaling used in the proof.  No spectral assertion for the ambient RFDE
generator is needed.

\subsection{Modal projectors}

With the notation of \cref{sec:model-main},
\[
 r=\vect{1\\2},\quad q=\vect{1\\-2},\quad
 \ell=\vect{1/2\\1/4},\quad m=\vect{1/2\\-1/4}.
\]
Direct multiplication gives
\[
 \ell^Tr=m^Tq=1,\qquad \ell^Tq=m^Tr=0,
\]
and therefore
\[
 P=r\ell^T=\mat{1/2&1/4\\1&1/2},\qquad
 P_\perp=qm^T=\mat{1/2&-1/4\\-1&1/2}.
\]
At \((v_*,w_*)\), the fast voltage derivative is
\[
 D_vF_{\rm FHN}(v_*,w_*)=\mat{-1&1/2\\2&-1}=-2P_\perp.
\]
Thus the critical and stable fast eigenvalues are \(0\) and \(-2\).
The cubic terms have Hessian
\[
 D_v^2F_{\rm FHN}(v_*,w_*)[\xi,\zeta]
 =\vect{-2s_*\xi_1\zeta_1\\0},
\]
so
\[
 \ell^TD_v^2F_{\rm FHN}(v_*,w_*)[r,r]=-s_*\ne0.
\]
Along \(v=v_*+rX\), the slow source is exactly
\[
 \vect{v_1-s_*-\mu\\v_2-2\mu}=r(X-\mu).
\]
These identities establish a one-dimensional nondegenerate fold and its
unfolding before any asymptotic reduction.

\subsection{Delay-layer identities}

The four entries depending on \(\eta\) are
\[
 \frac16+\eta,\qquad \frac16-2\eta,\qquad
 \frac13-\eta,\qquad \frac12+2\eta.
\]
Their strict positivity is equivalent to
\(-1/6<\eta<1/12\).  Since \(Tr=q\) and \(\ell^Tq=0\),
\begin{align*}
 C_0^\eta r&=\frac13r+\eta q,&
 C_1^\eta r&=\frac23r-\eta q,\\
 \ell^TC_0^\eta r&=\frac13,&
 \ell^TC_1^\eta r&=\frac23.
\end{align*}
It follows atom by atom that
\[
 \ell^T\mathbb B_\eta(\dd\theta)r
 =\frac13\delta_{\theta_0}(\dd\theta)
  +\frac23\delta_{\theta_1}(\dd\theta),
\]
whereas
\[
 P_\perp\mathbb B_\eta(\dd\theta)r
 =\eta q\bigl[\delta_{\theta_0}(\dd\theta)
              -\delta_{\theta_1}(\dd\theta)\bigr].
\]
Also \(Br=r\), so constant histories annihilate the delayed difference.

\subsection{Exact anisotropic scaling}

Let \(s=\del t\), \(\eps=\del^2\), and use
\cref{eq:blowup-scaling}.  The modal coordinates are recovered exactly as
\[
 X=\del^{-1}\ell^T(v-v_*),\qquad
 Z=\del^{-2}m^T(v-v_*),
\]
and similarly
\[
 Y=-\del^{-2}\ell^T(w-w_*),\qquad
 W=\del^{-4}m^T(w-w_*).
\]
The physical delay \(\theta_j/\del\) becomes the fixed translation
\(s\mapsto s-\theta_j\).  Taylor expansion is unnecessary because the local
field is polynomial.  Projecting the exact substituted equations with
\(\ell^T,m^T\) yields \cref{eq:exact-chart}.

The recovery scaling \(W=O(1)\) in chart variables is forced.  Indeed, the
fixed physical coupling gives
\[
 -d_wP_\perp(w-w_*)=-d_w\del^4qW,
\]
while the transverse component of the slow source is
\(\del^4qZ\).  Division by the chart time factor therefore gives
\(\del W'=Z-d_wW\), as in \cref{eq:exact-chart}.  An isotropic
\(O(\del^2)\) recovery scaling would instead leave a singular
\(-d_w/\del\) coefficient and obscure the invariant-graph balance.

\subsection{Exact model fitting}

Apply the shift \cref{eq:stable-shift}.  Because
\[
 \del\widehat Z'
 =\del Z'+\alpha\del X X',
 \qquad
 \del\widehat W'
 =\del W'+\frac{\alpha\del}{d_w}XX',
\]
substitution into \cref{eq:exact-chart} gives
\[
 q_0(X,Y)=\vect{Y-\alpha X^2\\-X},
 \qquad
 A=\mat{-2&0\\1&-d_w}.
\]
The singular constant terms in the stable equation cancel identically.
Every remaining monomial in both shifted equations contains at least one
factor of \(\del\).  Hence there are polynomial functions \(F,G\), with
only the fixed translations \(\theta_0,\theta_1\), for which
\cref{eq:special-flow-system} is an identity.

For \(d_w>0\),
\[
 \det(\lambda I-A)=(\lambda+2)(\lambda+d_w),
\]
so there are \(M_A,\beta>0\) such that
\(\|\e^{Ar}\|\le M_A\e^{-\beta r}\) for \(r\ge0\).  This is the only
linear spectral estimate used in the special-flow graph proof.  It concerns
the two shifted stable variables, not the full RFDE characteristic
operator.

Finally, direct collection at \(\del=0\) gives \cref{eq:Q1,eq:G0}.  The
computer-algebra implementation constructs the physical vector field and
delay matrices first, performs the scaling and projections, and verifies
that all four projected residuals and both reconstructed vector residuals
vanish identically.  The source file and exact tests are listed in
\cref{app:reproducibility}.
