# Suggested Journal of Nonlinear Science editors

Checked against the current official editorial-board page on 23 August 2026:
<https://link.springer.com/journal/332/editors>.

1. **Christian Kühn** — Technical University of Munich.  Best match for the
   main proof: multiscale dynamical systems, singular perturbations,
   bifurcations, scaling laws, and canards.  His current TUM profile describes
   a chair in Multiscale and Stochastic Dynamics:
   <https://www.professoren.tum.de/en/kuehn-christian>.

2. **Kyle Wedgwood** — University of Exeter.  Strong match for the model and
   biological interpretation: nonlinear dynamics, mathematical neuroscience,
   synaptic communication, and living-system models.  Exeter’s current group
   page lists him among the lead academics in Mathematics for Health and Life
   Sciences:
   <https://mathematics.exeter.ac.uk/research/health-life-sciences/>.

3. **Christian Bick** — Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam.  Strong match for the
   network/delay mechanism: coupled oscillators, network dynamical systems,
   higher-order and adaptive interactions, and delay-coupled oscillators:
   <https://www.math.vu.nl/~bick/>.

Before submission, the author should confirm that none has a recent
collaboration, institutional conflict, advisory relationship, or other
conflict of interest with the author.
