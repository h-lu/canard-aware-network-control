> **Long-Delay Shifts of Local Canard Connections in Retarded Fast–Slow Systems**

by Haibo Lu for publication as an Original Paper in the *Journal of
Nonlinear Science*.

The manuscript addresses a structural question for delayed fast–slow
systems: does the complete delayed operator visible after projection onto a
critical fold mode determine the canard connection threshold?  For a
two-module FitzHugh–Nagumo retarded functional differential equation, the
paper constructs a positive two-delay family whose total delayed gain and
complete critical projected delay measure are exactly fixed.  A transverse
delay channel nevertheless changes, returns nonlinearly to the critical
mode, and shifts a preparation-indexed complete-history connection by

\[
\mu_{c,\mathcal P}(\delta,\eta)-\mu_{c,\mathcal P}(\delta,0)
=\frac{K(\theta_0-\theta_1)}{\sqrt6}\,\delta^3\eta
+O(\delta^4|\eta|+\delta^3\eta^2).
\]

The main contribution is not the final implicit-function step.  The paper
constructs a complete-history invariant graph through a singular transverse
block, extends mixed parameter estimates to a logarithmically growing fold
tube, and uses explicit phase–normal one-sided Green operators to turn the
transverse return into a simple history-space connection root.  At the root,
the two retained RFDE histories coincide; no observable crossing is used.
The theorem is deliberately local and preparation-indexed.  Transfer to an
arbitrary physical outer Fenichel selection is stated only conditionally,
and no general-network or global spiking theorem is claimed.

We believe the paper fits the journal because it connects three audiences:
geometric singular perturbation and canard theory, infinite-dimensional
delay dynamics, and nonlinear network reduction.  Its identifiability
message extends beyond the displayed model: equality of a complete critical
projection need not determine a nonhyperbolic threshold when transverse
history channels can return through nonlinear geometry.  A reproducible
method-of-steps calculation supplies an independent sign and scale check but
is not used as proof.

The manuscript is original, has not been published, and is not under
consideration elsewhere.  The author has no competing interests.  A
versioned code-and-data archive accompanies the manuscript as supplementary
material.  All mathematical claims, computations, and the final text have
been reviewed and approved by the author.
